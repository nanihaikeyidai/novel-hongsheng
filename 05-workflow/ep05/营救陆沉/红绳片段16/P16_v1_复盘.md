## 二、失败归因（对回提示词原文）

### 问题 1｜红光泛滥 —— A 型（描述缺失）+ L 型（参考图污染）
- **触发句**（`金鱼_H3纯提示词_P16_v1.txt`）：
  - L4 `<Subject 3>`：`Faint distant RED alarm glow ONLY; main light cold blue-grey.`
  - L15 `<Picture 3>: fully_preserved - ... cold blue-grey with distant red`
  - L17：`Faint distant red pulses; main light cold fluorescent.`
- **原因**：三处都只写了「faint / distant / ONLY」，但**没有一条负向锁**说明红色**禁止**触及什么。参考图 v3 天花板正中有一枚红色警报灯，模型据此判定"红光是场景特征"，于是把它放大成环境照明：柱面砖红、车漆紫红、安娜侧脸与头发被染红。这是 `提示词避坑手册` 的 **A 型（描述缺失）** 叠加 **L 型（参考素材自带特征被放大）**。
- **改法（下版照抄）**：
  > `RED is limited to ONE small ceiling alarm lamp plus a thin red rim on ONE distant pillar. RED NEVER illuminates faces, hair, gloved hands, car paint or the floor. Every surface keeps its own pigment; only a 5% red edge is allowed. Key light is COLD fluorescent, cold blue-grey shadows.`

### 问题 2｜跑不起来 —— K 型（运动速率未定义，已到提示词效力上限）
- **触发句**：L18 `both sprint INTO the garage toward camera`；L19 `both sprint to the sedan`。
- **原因**：`sprint` 只是形容词，没有可数位移。与 P14 v6/v7 完全同一失效模式（当时 5 处锚点写满，仍是直立步态），说明 **H3 对"跑"的响应上限就在"快步走"**，继续加词无效。
- **改法**：**不再改提示词**。按既有配方走后期：`setpts=PTS/1.2~1.35` + `atempo` 同步，必要时 `tmix=frames=2:weights='3 1'` 增强动态。（本轮已产出 `_去红光`；加速版单独出。）

### 问题 3｜开场没有"撞门冲进" —— E 型（动作与机位冲突）
- **触发句**：L18 `At 00.000s wide low angle in <Picture 3>: the bottom fire door opens from the stairwell; Anna LEADS, Lu Chen behind, both sprint INTO the garage toward camera`
- **原因**：把「门被推开」写成一个**事件**，同时要求 `wide low angle in <Picture 3>`（参考图是没有人的空车库）。模型优先服从参考图的空场状态，于是先出 1.5s 空镜，人凭空出现在纵深里，没有入门动作。
- **改法**：不要描述"门打开"这个瞬时事件，改为**起点已含人**：
  > `At 00.000s wide low angle: Anna is ALREADY in the near foreground, mid-stride entering frame from the left fire door, body cut by the door frame, Lu Chen half a step behind her; the fire door is still swinging shut behind them.`

### 问题 4｜开门动作被特写吃掉 —— E 型（同镜双动作冲突）
- **触发句**：L20 `[Shot 3] HARD CUT to a TIGHT PROFILE CLOSE-UP of Anna ... she pulls the NEAR-SIDE FRONT PASSENGER DOOR open ...`
- **原因**：一个 2.5s 的特写镜头里同时要「侧脸特写」和「拉开车门」两个视觉任务，模型保留特写（信息密度高的那个）、丢掉开门（需要大幅肢体运动）。
- **改法**：拆开。特写只留「侧脸 + 台词 + 手已在门把上」；开门交给 Shot 2 收尾：
  > Shot 2 末追加 `her gloved hand lands on the passenger door handle and the door swings open at the very end of the shot`
  > Shot 3 改为 `her gloved hand already ON the open door edge, door standing open beside her`

### 问题 5｜车身紫红 —— 问题 1 的次级效应
- 已用后期压红（`p16_v1_grade_dered.py`：红色相 S×0.46 / V×0.96 + 全局 S×0.92 + 冷偏）救回大半，墙面砖红→灰粉，肤色自然。
- 剩余紫调仍在车漆上，下版靠问题 1 的负向锁从源头解决。

### 问题 6｜9.5s 弯腰进车头部糊 —— 模型能力边界
- **触发句**：L21 `[Shot 4] medium: Lu Chen ducks into the FRONT PASSENGER SEAT ... HARD CUT to Anna already IN THE DRIVER SEAT`
- **原因**：Shot 4 内部又要求一次 HARD CUT，模型在"弯腰"半途切机位，姿态过渡帧被抹成色块。
- **改法**：Shot 4 全段**一个机位到底**，取消镜内硬切；"安娜已系安全带"改用画外声（belt click）带过。

## 三、可直接复用的新约束块（建议回写避坑手册）

**约束块 11｜红色范围锁**
> `RED is confined to ONE small ceiling alarm lamp and a thin rim on ONE distant pillar. RED NEVER tints faces, hair, gloves, car paint or floor. Key light stays COLD fluorescent; shadows cold blue-grey.`

**约束块 12｜入口动作锁（不要写"门打开"）**
> `Character is ALREADY mid-stride inside the frame at 00.000s, body partially cut by the door frame, the door still swinging shut BEHIND them. Do NOT start on an empty establishing shot.`

**约束块 13｜同镜单动作锁**
> `One shot carries ONE dominant physical action. If a close-up is required, the body-changing action (opening a door, sitting down) must be completed in the PREVIOUS shot or start already finished.`
